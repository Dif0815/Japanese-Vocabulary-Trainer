DATA POLICY
-----------
vocabulary.csv is the authoritative vocabulary source.

Future vocabulary additions, corrections, grouping changes, conjugation corrections, and other
intentional CSV changes should be recorded in this file instead of README.txt.

Learning statistics are stored separately in the browser and are not part of this data-change log.
